## Decision

Four ideas from the logbook study are not built: (5) marking an entry when a file it names changed since its approval; (8) a structured handover note in four parts; (9) splitting a closed investigation into three lists; (11) a critical flag that orders the brief by the cost of being wrong.

## Reason

(5) Files change with every task, so nearly every entry would carry the mark and a person would have to clear it; check already reports a code pointer whose file or name is gone (R-CODE-3), and whoever edits the file is shown the entry first (D51). (8) What was decided, found and assumed belongs in entries, not in a note; the note stays one line of what is done and what is next (D54, D38). (9) solved together with found, propose and assume already gives the three lists, and review-task prints the investigation with the drafting rules (R-INV-8). (11) A person would have to judge every decision and flags inflate; standing decisions, digests (D22) and the check before an edit (D51) already bring the binding entries. Two more ideas wait as todo ideas: the git commit on a finding (6) and a check of staged files for confidential terms (10), which first needs a decision on where the terms of a public repository are kept.
