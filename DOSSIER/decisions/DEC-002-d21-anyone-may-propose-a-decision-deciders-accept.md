## Decision

A decision can be proposed by anyone, person or LLM. Only the project lead, or a person the lead names, accepts it.

## Reason

Three people and three LLMs must be able to suggest a direction without it becoming binding by accident. (Idea taken from decision-record tools such as MADR.)
