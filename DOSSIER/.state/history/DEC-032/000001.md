## Decision

Anyone in the project may add a confidential term, also an LLM when its person says so in the session; removing a term needs a person at a terminal. There are no automatic terms; writing to the team dossier is refused until the project has at least one term or a person stated that there are none. (Owner, 2026-11-21.)

## Reason

The tool cannot guess a customer's names; the refusal makes sure nobody forgets the list.
