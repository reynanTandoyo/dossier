## Decision

The rules for LLMs are split by role, as in todo. A common block of at most 8 lines is what every agent reads: `agents install` writes it into AGENTS.md, `help agents` prints it and the skill holds it. `help worker` (at most 7 lines) holds the rules of a worker: it writes nothing to the dossier and reports what it learned in its todo note. `help planner` holds every other rule. A test fails when the common block or the worker text grows past its limit. Claude Code hooks stay an extra, and there is no MCP server. (Owner, 2026-11-26.)

## Reason

Weak worker models follow long rules badly. The single rules text had 38 lines, about 1,070 tokens, and most of it is the business of the planner, so it could not be installed in a project that cheap workers work in.

## Rejected

One rules text with a part marked for workers: a worker would still have to read all of it.
